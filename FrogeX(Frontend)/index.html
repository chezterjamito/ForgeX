What ForgeX Does

🎲 Discover

Take the Discovery Quiz and find out which of six power categories you're stuck with — from elemental to bizarre.

💪 Train

Work through beginner-to-master training modules. Hard Training tiers unlock at higher levels and pay out serious XP.

🪪 Manage

Your Profile shows your power, your level and XP progress, and how many modules and incidents you've logged.

🌍 Adapt

Report real incidents involving powers — every report you file earns XP and helps the community track what's going on.

🏆 Compete

Public Leaderboard ranks every user by level and XP. Everyone with a public profile is on it.

🛠️ Govern

A small admin team manages the power catalog, training content, and incident review to keep ForgeX running smoothly.

How it works

1. Register and log in.
2. Take the Discovery Quiz — your answers weight which category your randomly-assigned power comes from.
3. Train your power through modules; harder tiers unlock as you level up.
4. Report incidents to earn extra XP and help keep society informed.
5. Check your Profile any time to see your standing, or the Leaderboard to see everyone else's.
